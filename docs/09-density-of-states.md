# Density of states

_Estimated time: 40 minutes | Difficulty: Intermediate | Status: planned_

---

This page will explain total DOS, atom-projected DOS and orbital-projected DOS, including how to name projections so that a reader can map every curve back to the structure. It will also cover energy alignment, k-point sampling and the difference between a numerical projection and a complete chemical assignment.

## Planned learning goals

- Build a projection table before plotting
- Align the energy axis consistently across materials
- Distinguish Ti, C and termination contributions without ambiguous labels
- Report the limits of projected-DOS interpretation
